The game

Why Did the Chicken Cross the Road is an endless hopper for the Arduboy. Your chicken hops forward, one tile at a time, through a world that is built as you go: grassy meadows with bushes, busy roads with cars and trucks, rivers with floating logs and sinking leaves, and even a runway where a plane crosses.

The screen keeps scrolling. Stay ahead of the edge: if the black catches up with you, the chicken bursts into feathers. Get hit, drown, or fall behind and it is game over.

Objective: get as far as you can and collect as much corn as possible.

Buttons

The title shows INFO · PLAY · CONF. Use Left and Right to highlight a word and A or B to confirm. INFO shows the credits, PLAY starts the game. In CONF press Left for sound on and Right for sound off.

D-pad
Hop one tile up, left, down or right. A hop pressed while the chicken is still in the air is remembered, so you can chain hops.
A / B
Pause, and press either button again to go on.
Game over
A goes back to the title, B plays again.

You

Chicken

That’s you. Hop forward, sideways or back, one tile at a time. Keep ahead of the black at the bottom of the screen.

On the road

Bush

Blocks your way on the grass. Hop around it.

Car

Crosses the road at speed. Get hit and it is over.

Truck

Longer and slower than a car, but just as deadly.

Log

Floats on the water. Hop on and ride it across the river.

Leaf

A stepping stone on the water. It sinks after a while, so keep moving.

Plane

Crosses the runway from left to right. Watch the windsocks.

Windsock

Limp when the runway is quiet, flying when a plane is coming.

Pickups

Corn

Worth 5 points.

Golden egg

An extra life.

Hourglass

The screen stops scrolling for about 5 seconds.

Magnet

Pulls in the corn around you for about 10 seconds.
